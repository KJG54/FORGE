# FORGE M6 Release Requirements

## Roadmap deliverables

The release-candidate evidence must cover:

- pre-v1 compatibility and migration behavior;
- supported CPython, operating-system, virtual-environment, and `pipx` installation cells;
- complete user, pack-author, adapter-author, architecture, security, troubleshooting, and
  recovery documentation;
- software and research example repositories;
- dependency, license, vulnerability, and secret review;
- maintained performance budgets;
- FORGE-governed dogfooding; and
- a release-candidate friction and residual-risk report.

## Exit criteria

M6 cannot be accepted until the closeout evidence establishes that:

- fresh users can complete both example workflows from built distributions;
- upgrade, backup, archive, abandonment, successor, and recovery procedures have been rehearsed;
- no critical governance, integrity, import, path, or secret defect remains;
- public compatibility commitments are documented; and
- the owner explicitly resolves every release-blocking risk.

## Increment 7 acceptance criteria

- The repository-local framework-change pack is declarative, Python-free, capability-free, and
  bound by its validated digest.
- `forge.yaml` configures that exact pack and enables clean-Git closure review.
- The real FORGE repository has one healthy active initiative using the locked framework-change
  workflow.
- This scope and these requirements are preserved as exact current artifact revisions.
- The `scope-reviewed` check and its evidence packet bind both revisions and the worker claim.
- The `scope` step reaches `awaiting_acceptance` but remains unaccepted until an explicit owner
  decision.

## Closeout evidence still required

Increment 8 must produce and govern:

- a manifest of the exact framework changes under review;
- complete local and remote release validation, including the supported build/install matrix;
- fresh-user and operational-procedure rehearsals;
- the final friction report and classified residual-risk register;
- explicit owner dispositions for every release-blocking risk;
- the M6 evidence report and readiness record; and
- lessons from release-candidate work.

These requirements do not authorize M7, public release, tagging, signing, or publication.
