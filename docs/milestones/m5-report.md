# Milestone 5 Evidence Report

**Milestone:** M5 — Research and Educational Proof

**Implementation state:** complete and owner-accepted

**Owner acceptance:** accepted in the Codex task by the repository owner on 2026-07-28

**Authorized boundary:** M5 only; M6 has not begun

## Outcome

M5 demonstrates that FORGE's lifecycle and authority model is not software-specific. The bundled
`research-basic` pack uses the same declarative loading, data trust, initiative creation, artifact
revision, claim, check, evidence, verification, acceptance, continuity, archival, and recovery
services as `software-basic`.

Research templates and structural validators remain digest-bound data. They can check declared
text structure but cannot establish source quality, methodology, semantic correctness, or factual
truth. Minimal, Standard, Guided, and Mentored explanations change educational detail only.
Long-gap resumption is derived from governed records without prior chat. Bounded filesystem
discovery suggests filenames under conservative limits without widening worker authority.

## Known limitations and deferred work

- FORGE remains a same-user governance and tamper-evidence framework, not a hostile-code sandbox.
- Structural research validation does not determine factual truth or methodological quality.
- Secret detection remains heuristic defense in depth.
- Discovery ranks bounded filename metadata rather than file semantics.
- SQLite FTS remains deferred because maintained measured scenarios did not show bounded discovery
  to be insufficient.
- Provider APIs, background execution, semantic retrieval, shared multi-user operation, and
  release-candidate support work remain outside M5.
- Naming, distribution metadata, support policy, performance budgets, examples, migrations, and
  release readiness belong to M6/M7.
